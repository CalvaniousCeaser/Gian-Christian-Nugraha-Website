About Me

I am Gian Christian Nugraha, a Chemistry Educator. I work in Sekolah Dian Harapan in Makassar, Indonesia. I used to work as the Secretary of the Lighthouse Singers (LHS) UPH. I went to the Karawachi Campus and studied Chemistry Education as my major. I graduated on March 26, 2026 and graduated as Bachelor of Education. My graduate thesis is about "The Implementation of Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, Project-Based Learning (STEM-PjBL) Model on Atomic Structure and Periodic Table Materials in Grade X of High School Z." I made this as my thesis because many High Schools curriculums often struggle with "Atomic Structure" and "The Periodic Table" concepts because they are highly abstract. So, I deployed an integrated a STEM Project-Based Learning (STEM-PjBL). Students were meant to work together in projects tailored around atomic science instead of static lectures. This changed classroom dynamics from teacher-centered lectures to student-led problem-solving. I am considered as a "fun and relaxing guy" by my peers and students. I am also noted for my high enthusiasm and high levels of dependability. I would also describe myself as a chill guy who loves teaching and hanging out with friends. I would also like to be like a pencil, because I want to leave marks–but marks that are good and meaningful.
